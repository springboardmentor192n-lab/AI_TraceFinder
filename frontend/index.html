Mission Overview

TraceFinder identifies which scanner was used to scan a document. Each scanner leaves tiny hidden noise patterns caused by hardware imperfections such as sensor defects, dust, and optical variation. These signatures can reveal the scanner brand, model, and likely source device instance.

"Just as cameras leave digital fingerprints and printers leave toner patterns, scanners leave their own source signatures."

Scanner Fingerprints

Extracts hidden noise patterns left behind by scanner sensors, optics, dust, and transport mechanics.

Brand and Model

Uses scanner signatures to estimate the source brand, model, and likely device family for uploaded scans.

Tampering Detection

Detects inconsistent compression, copy-move forgeries, and anomalous noise breaks across document regions.

Document Comparison

Determine if two documents share the same scanner origin — critical for chain-of-custody verification.

Batch Processing

Process dozens of documents simultaneously. Auto-groups results by inferred scanner device.

Forensic Reports

Court-ready PDF reports with executive summary, detection results, confidence metrics, and feature analysis.
